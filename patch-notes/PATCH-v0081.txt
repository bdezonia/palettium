Palettium v0081 - foreground recovery experiment
Apply after v0080. The VDD delivered one background and one foreground
notification despite a corrupted desktop. In PALXHOOK only, invalidate
cached bank selection and discard stale cursor saved-under on a new
foreground epoch at a guarded boundary. Add optional task-context
PALWATCH /repaint to request a desktop repaint. No ISR changes, assisted
save, palette writes or BIOS resets. Standard mode remains unchanged.
See docs/v0081-foreground-recovery.md; native validation required.
